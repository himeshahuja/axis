    Camera

    The webcam on the whiteboard, following the wrist wearing the band. Its track is the truth the band's own tracking is measured against. Each step ticks itself off.

    1. Turn on the camera
    2. Type the board's size
    3. Mark the board's corners
    4. Stand at the board, band on
    5. Mark the marker's tip (optional, better)

    Spectrum

    Writing moves below about 5 Hz; a hand's tremor shows at 6 to 12 Hz; above that is mostly the sensor's own noise. Top: acceleration, bottom: turning.

    Stillness check

    Hold the band still, as you would against the board between words, and press Measure. It shows whether the tracker would find a rest there, and what it would take.
