# VexoAI Hackathon — creative handoff

Product name: **Untitled demo** (working title only). Replace in `src/data/presentationDeck.ts` when the team agrees on a name.

## Product truth

A webcam follows the presenter's palm and maps its motion to a cursor on a mirrored presentation. Close the whole hand to grab a slide object or activate a control. Open the hand to release. Two held palm positions calibrate the movement range. Smaller range means faster cursor motion. Camera processing runs in the browser. Vexo wearable input is a future exploration, not a completed integration.

Hold a grasp still on a card to open Explain, Remember, and Voice. Explain currently summarizes the object's authored content locally. Remember saves a local snapshot. Voice opens an optional speech or typed-input panel. These are implemented choices; avoid implying an external AI explanation service is connected.

This is a prototype. Do not advertise measured accuracy, latency, user counts, universal compatibility, or integrated wearable support without evidence. Art is a campaign concept, not a product screenshot or photograph of the hackathon.

## Six-slide demo script

1. **The room is your canvas:** show the cursor, grasp a card, move and release.
2. **The laptop keeps pulling us back:** explain the interruption caused by returning to the keyboard.
3. **Close to grab. Open to release:** show a deliberate closed hand followed by opening.
4. **Your movement. Your sensitivity:** hold the two calibration positions.
5. **A whiteboard that moves with you:** rearrange Discover, Prototype, and Share live.
6. **Built for the next conversation:** distinguish today's camera prototype from the wearable roadmap, then invite someone to try it.

Suggested rehearsal: approximately three minutes. Keep a mouse nearby as a demo fallback. Do not claim calibration eliminates all tracking error.
