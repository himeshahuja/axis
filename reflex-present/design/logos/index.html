Axis — 10 logo concepts

Each shown on the pitch's black with the orange accent, and in one colour on white.

01 Crosshair A

An A with no crossbar: the point you aim at holds it together.

02 Inverse X

Wordmark with the X set in a solid block — the selected object. The cursor waits after it.

03 Layered A

An A cut into layers, like a scan line; the selected layer is the crossbar.

04 Three axes

X, Y and the third axis — toward you — meeting at one point.

05 Crossed slabs

Two slabs cross into an X; one passes over the other — depth, not a flat screen.

06 Pinch

Thumb and finger meeting: the pinch that grabs, drawn as an A.

07 Radial

The hold-and-turn menu as the mark; the top segment is lit.

08 Rule

A heavy wordmark beside one orange rule, with the line underneath.

09 Pixel X

An X on a grid of cells; the centre cell is the one you're pointing at.

10 Point i

Lowercase wordmark; the i's dot is the point, with the band's ring around it.
