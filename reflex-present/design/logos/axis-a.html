Axis — the bar A, boxed, then XIS

Five takes on one decision: a stepped bar A with an orange crossbar, in a box, followed by XIS. Each shown as the full lockup and as the box alone at icon sizes, on black and on white.

01 Solid box

The A cut out of a solid square; XIS in DM Sans Bold, the pitch’s body face.

02 Outline box

A drawn frame around the bars; XIS in a monospace for a technical, tool-like read.

03 App tile

Rounded square, ready as an app icon on its own; XIS in Gravitas One, the pitch’s display face.

04 Viewfinder

Only the box’s corners — a selection around the A, the thing you point at; XIS light and spaced.

05 Breakout

The orange bar runs out of the box and into the word: the A reaches into XIS.
