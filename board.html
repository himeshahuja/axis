Board

Wear the band on your writing hand. Once it is connected and still, knock the whiteboard's left edge, then its far right edge: from then on the page shows where your hand is on the board. Teach it Take and Put, and you can lift writing off the board and drop it on this screen. Nothing showing? The bench shows what the band sends.

Connect does nothing? This page was opened as a file (by double-click), so the browser blocks its program. In a terminal, in this repository's folder, run python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1, then open http://localhost:8000/board.html in Chrome or Edge.

Camera

The camera stops the cursor drifting. It finds you in the picture and follows the wrist wearing the band. Five steps, once per board: each ticks itself off.

  1. Turn on the camera
  2. Type the board's size
  3. Mark the board's corners
  4. Stand at the board, band on
  5. Mark the marker's tip (optional, better)

Measure

How far off is the cursor on a real board? Put a few dots on it and measure each from the spot where you tapped the left edge: x along the board, y up, in cm. Turn on "Measure taps" and tap the dots in order.
